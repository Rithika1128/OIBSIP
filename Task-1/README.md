# DataAnalytics-L1-EDARetailSales

This folder contains my work for Task 1 of the Oasis Infobyte Data Analytics Internship.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to understand sales performance, profit, customer demographics, product categories, geographic markets, and sales trends over time.

The analysis was carried out using Python, Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib to clean the dataset, explore patterns, and identify useful business insights.

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib

## Key Skills

- Data Cleaning
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Data Visualization

## Dataset

The dataset contains retail sales information, including:

- Date-related information
- Customer age and gender
- Country and state
- Product category and sub-category
- Product name
- Order quantity
- Unit cost and unit price
- Profit, cost, and revenue

The dataset originally contained 113,036 rows and 18 columns. After removing 1,000 duplicate records, the cleaned dataset contained 112,036 rows and 18 columns.

## Analysis Performed

The project includes:

- Data loading and initial inspection
- Data cleaning and duplicate removal
- Descriptive statistics
- Sales and profit analysis
- Product category analysis
- Country and regional analysis
- Customer age and gender analysis
- Yearly and monthly sales analysis
- Correlation analysis
- Top-performing products and regions
- Data visualization using Matplotlib

## Key Findings

- Bikes generated the highest revenue, at approximately **61.43 million**.
- Accessories had the highest profit margin, at approximately **58.63%**.
- The United States generated the highest country-level revenue, at approximately **27.78 million**.
- 2015 recorded the highest yearly revenue, at approximately **19.95 million**.
- The 35-64 age group generated the highest revenue, at approximately **42.37 million**.
- California was the highest-revenue region among the top 10 regions analyzed.

## Business Recommendations

- Focus on maintaining strong sales of Bikes since they generated the highest revenue.
- Promote Accessories to make better use of their high profit margin.
- Study customer preferences in the United States to understand the factors behind its strong sales.
- Analyze the performance of California to identify strategies that may work in other regions.
- Consider targeted marketing for the 35-64 age group based on its contribution to revenue.

## Conclusion

This project helped me understand how retail sales data can be cleaned, analyzed, and visualized using Python. The analysis provided useful insights into products, customers, regions, and sales trends, and showed how data can be used to understand business performance.
